# Credits and licensing

Path Keeper is an unofficial, non-commercial module for Foundry Virtual Tabletop. It contains no artwork, and no game rules text beyond a few cited rules adjustments and short fragments of rules wording its readers match, all ORC Licensed Material credited in [ORC-NOTICE.md](../ORC-NOTICE.md), which ships with the module. Everything it shows about ancestries, heritages, backgrounds, classes, feats, spells, and the rest — names, descriptions, icons — is read at runtime from the Pathfinder Second Edition and Starfinder Second Edition game systems installed in your Foundry world, and the credit for that content belongs to those systems and to Paizo.

The screenshots in the Player and GM guides show Foundry and Path Keeper's interface, captured on a blank canvas. Every image the game data supplies is replaced when the screenshot is taken: the PF2e/SF2e systems' portraits, class emblems, and item icons by a plain placeholder, and sheet background art by a flat fill of its average colour (all of it Paizo's artwork or art licensed to those systems alone), along with any other module's art. The capture fails if any such image remains. The names and rules text visible in them are the installed system's data.
