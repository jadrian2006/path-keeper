# Troubleshooting

[← Player Guide contents](README.md)

## My sandbox character or promotion has not appeared

- A GM must be online for the request to be processed. Your request is kept on your account and handled when a GM logs in.
- You are notified when a request is built, sent back, or fails. A sent-back request includes the GM's note.
- Sandbox characters may be turned off, or you may have reached the per-player limit. Delete an old sandbox character from the Actors directory to make room.
## An option I expect is missing

- Your GM chooses which books and rarities are allowed. Only the core rulebooks are on by default. Ask your GM about anything else.
- Heritages only list those for the chosen ancestry, plus versatile heritages.

## An option is marked "review" and I cannot create the character

The option's rarity is set to **review**, it has prerequisites Path Keeper cannot check automatically, or it comes from a world or module compendium your GM has not trusted. You can still send the plan: your GM can allow it on your request, trust its compendium, or add it on the sheet after promotion.

## PF2e still asked me a question during creation

The Review step warned about this choice: it belongs to an item granted several steps removed from anything Path Keeper creates, so it could not be answered in advance. Answer PF2e's prompt as usual. See [PF2e rule choices](03-pf2e-rule-choices.md).
